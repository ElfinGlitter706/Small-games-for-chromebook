HOW TO PLAY

Goal: keep climbing and get the highest score possible.

Controls: use the LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys. Your character automatically bounces when landing.

Solid platform
Safe to land on. It stays forever.
Break platform
Works for one landing, then disappears.
Spring platform
Gives you a much stronger bounce and launches you higher.
Horizontal mover
Slides left and right. Watch the path lines to time your landing.
Vertical mover
Glides up and down. Path lines show its travel range.

Collect the floating coins as you climb. New cosmetics can be bought from the Customize menu.

Moving platforms appear after you climb higher (score ~600+).

SETTINGS

Higher sensitivity makes horizontal movement faster.
