# DEADSHIFT

[Play DEADSHIFT in your browser](https://elfinglitter706.github.io/Small-games-for-chromebook/DEADSHIFT/)

Choose The Outpost for endless survival or Vertigo Tower to fight your way to the rooftop helicopter. Use Chrome or another WebGL 2 browser on a computer with a keyboard and mouse.

Move with WASD, fire with the left mouse button, and hold the right mouse button to aim down the sights. Press E to interact, R to reload, Q to swap weapons, and Escape to pause.

Use the arrow keys for camera control, P to toggle performance stats, and G to use carried equipment. You can change these bindings under Controls. Solo runs save automatically after eligible cleared waves. Choose Continue run on the main menu to resume.

On The Outpost, restore power and call the relief train from Transit Depot. Survive three full waves after the call, then board at the platform. On Vertigo, watch for changing room conditions as you climb. Training and pre-run challenge modifiers are available from the main menu and settings.

Open **Armory, settings & records → Armory** to customize each gun. Gun kills earn 20 weapon XP and headshot kills earn 30. The next five weapon levels cost 100, 250, 500, 850, and 1,300 additional XP. Levels 2 through 5 each unlock one attachment slot. Level 6 unlocks the final attachment choice. Equipped parts apply whenever you acquire that gun. Browser progress is saved on this device in this browser profile.

The Godot source, export presets, and playtests are under `project/`. Open `project/project.godot` with Godot 4.7.2 to edit the game.
